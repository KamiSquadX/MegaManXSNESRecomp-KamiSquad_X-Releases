# KSX Zero donor assets

KSX Zero builds on Saber Zero by RaphaelAzev, whose saber sprites and sounds
come from the Zashiko Mod (see `assets/saber-zero/CREDITS.md`).

The Special Move and Giga Attack sheets in `sprites/` are built by
`tools/saber/build_ksx_zero_sheets.py` from two fan projects:

* **Mega Man X Engine** by Dark Shadow (Dragon-Developer) and team
  (https://github.com/Dragon-Developer/Mega-Man-X-Engine): Ryuenjin,
  Hyouretsuzan, Raikousen (ground and air), Mikazukizan (used for Kuuenbu)
  and Messenko with its shards (used for Rakuhouha). Zero sprites by Thomas Light (MaverickBlue); effects by SuperKuperX
  and the engine's spriting team.
* **X8 16-bit** by Alysson da Paz: Youdantotsu and Juuhazan, with Juuhazan's
  slash effect. X8 16-bit is free to use, modify and distribute but not to be
  monetized in any way; its credits and Alysson da Paz's signature must be kept
  with any derivative work.

The pause-menu icons in `icons/` are built by
`tools/saber/build_ksx_zero_icons.py`: Ryuenjin, Rakuhouha, Kuuenbu and
Hyouretsuzan are Capcom's Mega Man X4 weapon-menu icons, cut from a capture
of that menu; Raikousen, Youdantotsu, Juuhazan and Rekkyoudan are X8 16-bit's
Zero pause icons (same terms as above). Each is fitted to X1's 12x12 icon
inside.

The sheets share one palette. Juuhazan's purple blade and Mikazukizan's
orange saber layer (which the engine tints in game) are recoloured to the
standard green so that Rekkyoudan can turn every saber purple the same way.

This project is not monetized. Do not use these assets commercially.

ROMs are never included; players provide their own Mega Man X USA and Mega Man X3 USA ROMs.
